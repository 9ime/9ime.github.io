I am an audio AI researcher and engineer working at the intersection of generative audio, spatial audio, and real-time music applications. My recent work focuses on AI-assisted spatial audio production, on-device music generation and deployment, music wellness applications, sound effect design, and bringing audio models into practical product environments.Before my current role, I worked on speech synthesis, voice conversion, and end-to-end music generation, which shaped my broader interest in controllable, expressive, and deployable audio generation systems.
